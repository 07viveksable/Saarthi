Getting Started
Common questions for new users
What is SAARTHI and how does it work?
SAARTHI is India's AI-powered welfare guide that helps you discover government schemes you are eligible for, guides you through the application process step by step, and tracks your application status — all in your local language. It works on any phone or computer with a browser.
Which languages does SAARTHI support?
SAARTHI currently supports Hindi, Marathi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, and Gujarati. You can change your language any time from the Settings page. Voice input is available in all supported languages.
Is SAARTHI free to use?
Yes, SAARTHI is completely free for all citizens. There are no hidden charges, subscription fees or service charges. SAARTHI is a government-supported initiative to ensure every eligible citizen receives their entitled benefits.
How do I update my profile details?
Go to Settings from the left sidebar. Under "Personal Information" you can update your name, mobile number, address and other profile details. Changes are saved automatically and reflected in your active applications.
Applications & Documents
Questions about applying and uploading documents
How do I check my application status?
Open the Application Tracker from the left sidebar. You will see all your applications with their current status, progress bar, and a full step-by-step timeline. SAARTHI syncs with government portals to show the latest status automatically.
What file formats are accepted for document upload?
SAARTHI accepts JPG, PNG, PDF and HEIC formats. Maximum file size is 10MB per document. For best results, take photos in good natural light on a flat surface. The AI will automatically read and verify key details from your documents.
My application was rejected. What should I do?
First check the reason for rejection in your Application Tracker. Most rejections are due to missing or incorrect documents. You can re-upload the correct document from the Document Vault and SAARTHI will guide you to re-apply. If you need help, use the Contact Helpline above.
How long does it take for applications to be processed?
Processing time varies by scheme. Ayushman Bharat cards are typically issued in 3–7 days. PM-KISAN instalments take 2–4 weeks after Aadhaar-bank seeding verification. PM Awas Yojana can take 3–6 months due to field verification. SAARTHI shows expected timelines in your Application Tracker.
Privacy & Security
Your data is safe with us
How does SAARTHI protect my personal data?
All documents and personal data are encrypted using AES-256 — the same standard used by banks. Data is stored on Indian servers only, under IT Act 2000 compliance. Your Aadhaar number is never stored in full — only a masked version (XXXX-XXXX-1234) is retained. Your documents are never shared without your explicit consent.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes. Go to Settings and scroll to "Account" at the bottom. You can submit a data deletion request. All your personal data and documents will be permanently deleted within 30 days as per Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
